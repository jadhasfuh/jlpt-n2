jlptest is a web app for studying Japanese, aimed at learners preparing for
the JLPT proficiency exam (levels N5 to N1). It provides vocabulary, kanji,
grammar, graded reading passages and practice tests, together with a spaced
repetition system that tracks each learner's own progress.

Part of the app is free and needs no account. Full access to all five levels
is sold as a monthly subscription of MX$79, billed in Mexican pesos and
renewing automatically until the customer cancels. Customers can cancel at any
time from their profile and keep access until the end of the period they have
already paid for. If a customer is not satisfied we refund that period on
request within 14 days.

All study material is written by us. Delivery is instant and entirely on the
web at https://jlptest.org — there is nothing to ship and no physical goods
are involved.

We are not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by the organisers of the
JLPT. The exam name is used only to describe which exam the material prepares
learners for.

The app carries no advertising and no third-party tracking, and we do not sell
or share personal data.
