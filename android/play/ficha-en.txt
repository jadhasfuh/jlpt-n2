SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Japanese for the JLPT: vocabulary, kanji, grammar and mock exams, N5 to N1.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Study Japanese for the JLPT with a course that runs in order from beginning to end, N5 through N1.

WHAT'S INSIDE
· 8,027 words arranged into 614 themed units, not loose lists.
· 1,981 kanji with their readings, stroke counts and the words they appear in.
· 846 grammar points, from the simplest to the most complex.
· 614 reading passages written to level: each one uses only the vocabulary and kanji you have already seen.
· Practice exams with the structure and the scoring of the real JLPT.

HOW YOU STUDY
· Spaced repetition flashcards: each word comes back just as you are about to forget it.
· A test for every unit, with a medal for your best score.
· Listening exercises: hear the sentence without seeing it, then answer.
· Rebuild the sentence piece by piece, with the translation beside it.
· Furigana you can switch on and off, and kanji coloured by level.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The whole interface and every translation is in English and Spanish. The definitions are our own, not copied.

YOUR PROGRESS
A day streak, XP, mastered words and a ring for each level and topic showing how far you have come. Progress is saved to your account and follows you from one device to another.

You can start for free: the 人と体 section of every level is open.
